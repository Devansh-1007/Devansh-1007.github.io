Privacy policy: n8n JobHunt

Last updated: 11 October 2026

Who uses this app

n8n JobHunt is a personal automation built and used only by Devansh Choudhary. It runs in a self-hosted n8n instance on his own computer. It is not offered to the public.

What data it accesses

  • Gmail: reads job-alert emails in the owner's own mailbox to extract job titles, companies and links, and creates email drafts that the owner reviews and sends himself.
  • Google Sheets: reads and writes the owner's own job-tracker spreadsheets.

How data is used

Job listing text is used to build the owner's job-application tracker. Short job descriptions may be sent to the Google Gemini API to score how well a listing matches the owner's profile.

Sharing

Data is not sold, rented or shared with third parties. It stays in the owner's Google account and on his own computer. The only exception is a leads tracker sheet that the owner chooses to share with people he invites.

Retention and deletion

Data stays in the owner's Google account until he deletes it. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
